How to Play

Goal: Find the ✨ Gold and return to the start cell without dying!

Percepts

  • 🌬️ Breeze: A Pit is in an adjacent cell.
  • 🤢 Stench: The Wumpus is in an adjacent cell.
  • ✨ Glitter: The Gold is in this cell!

Hazards

Stepping into a Pit or the Wumpus cell means instant death.
